The collection

Sovereign is the house's first full winter. Quiet authority, cut for the long evenings: double-face wool, dense knit, leather finished with lace.

A London house, worn in rather than worn out.

Story

Founded by Angelica in Altrincham, Morganà dresses the woman who already knows the room. Wine-lacquered doors, walnut, sconces: old-world London, taken out in the evening.

— Morganà

II — Look

The slip, the jacket, and nothing in between.

Champagne lace against brushed tweed. A single button. Pieces that sit on the body the way a room sits around you.

Dress for the door you are walking through, not the one you left. — Morganà
