1Look one · The long coat

The longcoat.

Floor-sweeping and belted, with lapels wide enough to turn up against the wind. The piece the house began with, cut again for the second winter. Under it, the roll-neck does the quiet work.

2Look two · Lace, after dark

Lace,afterdark.

A fitted knit slip, edged in champagne lace at the neckline and hem. Worn with a sheer stocking and one piece of gold. The wine doors do the rest.

3Look three · Knit & walnut

Knit&walnut.

Open-knit edged in tan suede, a fine roll-neck and a button-front midi in espresso. The long evenings in, with the lamps low.
