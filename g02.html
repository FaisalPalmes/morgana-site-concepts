Programme notes

The Sovereign Season

The house opens its doors on the longest evenings of the year. Sovereign is a wardrobe for them: tailoring with a straight back, knit that keeps its counsel, leather and lace for after the lights go down. Twenty pieces, cut in London, worn as one would wear a season ticket.

The programme runs in three acts. Each is a room of the house, and each piece appears in its own role. Prices are given in the cast list, and every seat is a good one.

— Morganà
