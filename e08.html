Story

Morganà was founded by Angelica with a single idea: that a woman should dress for the room she is already in. Wine-lacquered doors, walnut, sconces. London interiors, worn out in the evening.

Sovereign is the house's second winter and its first full one: double-face wool, dense knit, leather finished with lace. Twenty pieces, each named later by Angelica herself.

— Morganà
