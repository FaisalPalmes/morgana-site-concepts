About the
exhibition

Sovereign is the house's third chapter, hung in three rooms. The first is black: the doors, the long coat, the fur collar. The second is the rail, filmed the morning before the doors open. The third is wine, the colour of the salon itself. Every work is for sale and leaves with you.

— Morganà, London
