Wall textSovereign

Twenty works in tailoring, knit and leather, hung in three rooms: the wine-lacquered doors, the salon, and the atelier. Each is cut for long evenings and a quiet kind of authority.

Every work is available in edition. Sizes XS to XL. Nothing on these walls is a print; what you see hanging is what you take home.

— Morganà

The screening room

Three films · silent · looped

Shot in one evening, in the house. The doors are lacquered in wine; the light is the sconces and nothing else.

Each reel runs under a minute. Sound is left to the room you watch it in.
